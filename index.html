Frequently asked questions

Is SafeBite a substitute for medical advice?+
No. SafeBite is a risk-awareness tool, not a medical device. Always carry your prescribed epinephrine and consult your allergist for personal dietary decisions.
How does the scanner actually work?+
It runs real optical character recognition (OCR) on your uploaded photo to extract actual text, then checks that text against the FDA's official list of 9 major food allergens. It does not guess a random dish — it only reports what it can actually read.
Where does the restaurant data come from?+
Restaurant names and addresses come live from OpenStreetMap's public database for the zip code you search. We do not display fabricated allergy scores — those only appear once real community reports exist.
Why don't restaurants show a safety score yet?+
We only show a score when it's backed by real ingredient data or real diner-submitted reports. Since this is an early prototype, most restaurants will show "no reports yet" rather than a fabricated number.
Is SafeBite free?+
Yes, the core scanner and restaurant search will be free at launch.
