Frequently asked questions

Is SafeBite a substitute for medical advice?+
No. SafeBite is a risk-awareness tool, not a medical device. Always carry your prescribed epinephrine and consult your allergist for personal dietary decisions, and confirm with restaurant staff before ordering.
Where does the allergen menu data come from?+
Menu allergen data is sourced from each chain's official, publicly published allergen guide. We currently cover 25 major national chains as our Phase 1 dataset, with more being added over time.
What if my restaurant isn't in the database yet?+
We only show verified allergen matches for chains in our current database. Restaurants outside it show "no verified allergen data yet" rather than a fabricated score — we'd rather be honest than guess.
Why did we drop the photo scanner?+
Photo-based menu scanning proved unreliable for dense, multi-column menus. We pivoted to structured, verified allergen data instead, which gives more accurate and trustworthy results.
Is SafeBite free?+
Yes, the core restaurant and menu search is free at launch.
